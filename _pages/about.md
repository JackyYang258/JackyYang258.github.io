Hello! I'm **Siqi Yang** (pronounced /schee/, 杨思琪 in Chinese). I am currently pursuing my **MSCS** (research track) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)](https://illinois.edu/), where I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Yu-Xiong Wang](https://yxw.cs.illinois.edu/).<br><br>My research centers on **coding agents** and **self-evolving AI systems**: how the harness around a model — its prompts, tools, memory, and workflow — can be designed, evolved, and analyzed, and what such an evolved artifact actually encodes. My work spans **agent harness design**, **reinforcement learning**, and **LLM post-training**. In summer 2026 I was a research intern at [IBM Research](https://research.ibm.com/) in Yorktown Heights, where I built an instrumented self-evolving coding-agent harness and studied how its gains split between language-specific and model-specific compensation.<br><br>Before joining UIUC, I completed my B.Eng in Electronic Engineering at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), where I worked with [Prof. Huazhe Xu](http://hxu.rocks/) and [Prof. Yu Wang](https://web.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/wangyu/en/index.htm) on robot learning and imitation learning.<br><br>I have been a teaching assistant for CS 598 (ML for Software Engineering, Fall 2025) and CS 527 (Topics in Software Engineering, Spring 2026) at UIUC, and a reviewer for ICML 2026 and ICPR 2026.
